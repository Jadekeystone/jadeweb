Jade Keystone Solutions and Paints & Coatings Skill Council Join Hands to Strengthen India's Skilling Ecosystem

October 2025
MoU Signing Ceremony

Official partnership agreement between Jade Keystone Solutions and PCSC

Strategic Planning

Leadership teams discussing collaboration opportunities

Mumbai, October 2025: Jade Keystone Solutions Pvt. Ltd. (JKSPL) and the Paints & Coatings Skill Council (PCSC) have signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) to collaborate on advancing skill development, industry readiness, and innovation in the paints and coatings sector.

The partnership aims to bring together industry insight, training expertise, and communication outreach to empower youth, workers, and enterprises through structured programs and digital initiatives. Under this collaboration, both organizations will jointly undertake initiatives in awareness, advocacy, technology integration, and employment enablement, aligning with the goals of the Skill India Mission.

A key focus area under this MoU is the launch of the "Skill & Sector" podcast series, featuring conversations with industry leaders, trainers, and entrepreneurs – designed to inspire and showcase India's evolving skilling landscape.

"This partnership reflects our shared vision of creating meaningful pathways for skill-based growth and entrepreneurship. Together with PCSC, we aim to communicate the power of skills that shape industries, livelihoods, and India's future."

Mr. Narendra Deshpande, Director of Jade Keystone Solutions

"Through this collaboration, we will highlight the transformative role of skilling in the paints and coatings sector and create a sustainable platform for industry – academia – youth engagement."

Mr. Jagdish Acharya, CEO of Paints & Coatings Skill Council

The MoU opens new avenues for cooperation in training innovation, storytelling, and digital advocacy, helping make the sector more aspirational, inclusive, and future-ready.

Key initiatives under this partnership include:

  • Development of industry-relevant training modules
  • Digital learning platforms for remote skill development
  • Industry-academia collaboration programs
  • Employment linkage and placement support
  • Research and development in skill assessment methodologies

Both organizations will work closely to identify skill gaps, develop competency frameworks, and create career progression pathways for professionals in the paints and coatings industry.
