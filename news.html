Jade Keystone Solutions and Paints & Coatings Skill Council Join Hands

October 2025

Mumbai, October 2025: Jade Keystone Solutions Pvt. Ltd. (JKSPL) and the Paints & Coatings Skill Council (PCSC) have signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) to collaborate on advancing skill development, industry readiness, and innovation in the paints and coatings sector.

The partnership aims to bring together industry insight, training expertise, and communication outreach to empower youth, workers, and enterprises through structured programs and digital initiatives.
